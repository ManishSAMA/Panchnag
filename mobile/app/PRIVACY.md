# Jain Panchang: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: <date of publishing>*

Jain Panchang (जैन पंचांग) is a free Jain calendar app for Android, published by
<developer name as shown on Google Play>.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not send
any data off your phone.

Every panchang calculation (tithi, sunrise, choghadiya, pachchakkhan times,
festivals) runs on your phone without an internet connection. The city you
choose and your settings (language, tradition, sunrise rule and similar) are
stored only on your phone. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or
uninstall it.

## Crash reports

This version does not send crash reports. If a later version adds crash
reporting, it will be off unless you switch it on, and this policy will be
updated before that version is released to say what is sent and to whom.

## Reporting a wrong date

If you use the "report if something looks wrong" action, the app opens your own
email or WhatsApp with a pre-filled message: the date, location, time zone,
settings and app version. Nothing is sent unless you send that message
yourself.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
